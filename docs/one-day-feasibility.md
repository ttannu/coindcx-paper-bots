# Can one ₹5,000 CoinDCX wallet earn ₹10,000 in 24 hours?

**No strategy in the available evidence supports that promise.** The requested profit means a ₹15,000 final wallet value, or a 200% net return in one day. This screen was run on 3 October 2026, before the user expects to provide account access on 4 October. It places no orders and uses no CoinDCX credentials. The existing scheduled paper challenge still lasts 15 days; it must not be mistaken for a successful one-day test.

The [fixed plan](../research/one_day_plan.md) starts a fresh ₹5,000 wallet at 00:00 UTC in each of **255 non-overlapping 24-hour windows**, from 18 January through 29 September 2026. The four previously fixed BTC/INR, ETH/INR, SOL/INR and XRP/INR spot rules are compared with cash and two holding controls. They use only completed hourly candles and the existing regular CoinDCX spot-fee, spread, GST, TDS and estimated-tax model. The configuration assumes a 0.5% basic INR spot fee per side plus 18% GST on the fee, pair-specific spread measured on 30 September, and 31.2% estimated tax on positive VDA gains; [CoinDCX's published tiers](https://coindcx.com/vip/) and the [Income-tax Act, 2025, section 194](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf) inform those assumptions. The main screen prices orders at the next hour's open; a sensitivity test delays execution to the next hour's close. A target hit at any hourly mark values open positions after hypothetical exit costs and tax.

**A deliberately unfair hindsight comparison still missed the daily target.** At each UTC-day boundary, an oracle picked the best of the 47 INR coins in the existing universe, as if it knew tomorrow's winner before buying. Its best day was SIREN: **+129.1% in the coin price**, or **+85.6%** for one ₹5,000 modeled spot wallet after costs. No UTC day had a coin price rise of 200%, and no modeled wallet reached ₹15,000. These coins were selected at the *end* of the historical period, which makes the oracle more optimistic than a real strategy.

Looking at **every hourly starting point** found one 24-hour coin-price gain above 200% among 284,652 overlapping pair-and-start observations: ONE rose **217.0%** starting at 23:00 UTC on 16 September. Buying it at the first candle close and selling at the last, with perfect advance knowledge, leaves only **₹12,148.54** in the modeled wallet after costs and estimated tax. Its endpoint candles had positive reported volume, but that does not prove a ₹5,000 order could fill at either close. The many overlapping observations cannot be read as independent trials or a probability forecast. Because this was the only gross 200% move, no rolling 24-hour single-coin spot hold in the archive met a 200% *net* target under the model's positive trading costs.

These are candle screens, not executable returns: no historical order-book depth, queue position, exchange outages or real account-specific fees were observed. The user’s tax jurisdiction and actual fee tier are unconfirmed. The spot rules were fixed before this one-day screen but the broader market period had already been examined, so this is not untouched out-of-sample evidence. The oracle knows future winners and cannot be automated honestly. Leveraged futures could turn a smaller favorable price move into a large paper gain, but also magnify adverse moves and can liquidate the margin; the repository's futures paper results use INR *spot* candles as a proxy and fixed funding, so they cannot validate a CoinDCX futures edge. CoinDCX also [warns that leverage magnifies losses and liquidation risk](https://coindcx.com/blog/crypto-futures-trading/crypto-futures-trading-risk-management/).
