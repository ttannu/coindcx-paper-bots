# One-day ₹10,000 profit feasibility check — fixed before running

The user's revised goal is to turn one ₹5,000 stake into at least ₹15,000 within 24 hours. No rule can guarantee that result. This is a research screen, not permission to place real orders.

Use the four BTC/INR, ETH/INR, SOL/INR and XRP/INR spot rules already fixed in `strategy_plan.md`, plus its cash, BTC-hold and equal-hold controls. Start each independent 24-hour window with one ₹5,000 wallet at 00:00 UTC. Use every non-overlapping day from 18 January through 29 September 2026 (255 windows), after the same warm-up history and with the same fee, spread, GST, TDS and estimated-tax model as the 15-day screen. Report mean, median, best and worst after-cost value, profitable days, and days reaching ₹15,000 at any hourly mark or by the end. Run both the next-open fill model and the more delayed next-close sensitivity. No strategy parameters will be selected from the outcome.

Separately scan the 47 INR coins fixed in `config.json` for a *hindsight* 24-hour single-coin spot hold. At each UTC-day boundary, compare prices exactly 24 hours apart where both hourly candles exist. Report the best gross price move, the number of days when **any** coin rose 200% or more, and the number when buying that coin at the first close and selling at the last close would have reached ₹15,000 under the same modeled costs. Also report the largest return over every rolling 24-hour pair of hourly closes, so a midnight boundary cannot hide an intraday move. This oracle knows the winner in advance, ignores order-book depth, and uses coins selected at the end of the period; it is context, not a deployable strategy or an upper bound on leveraged trading.

The historical archive is kept locally. Publish the code, data digest, aggregate results and limits, without any exchange credential or live-order capability. Do not change the existing 15-day paper challenge's state or runtime schedule to make it appear that it met a one-day target.
