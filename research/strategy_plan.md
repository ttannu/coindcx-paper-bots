# Predeclared CoinDCX strategy screen — 3 October 2026

The user's target is **₹10,000 profit from ₹5,000**, meaning a final value of **₹15,000**. The existing paper challenge lasts 15 days, so this screen uses that horizon while the user clarifies their desired timeframe. No rule can assure a gain, let alone a 200% gain, in every market.

The candidate rules below were fixed before running `strategy_screen.py`. This is a single ₹5,000 spot wallet per rule and window; four bots do not create four times the user's capital. The four pairs, BTC/INR, ETH/INR, SOL/INR and XRP/INR, were chosen for continuous hourly history and comparatively deep INR trading, without picking past winners. The archival CoinDCX candles cover 17 non-overlapping 15-day windows from 18 January to 30 September 2026. All rules start each window in cash and can use only candles closed before an order.

| Rule | Fixed signal and position sizing | Recheck |
|---|---|---|
| BTC SMA 30 | Hold 95% BTC while above its trailing 30-day average; otherwise cash. This overlaps prior research and is a control for trend timing. | Daily |
| Time-series momentum 30 | Buy BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP at 25% of current wallet value each when a coin's 30-day return is positive and it is above its 30-day average; otherwise cash. Held weights can drift until an exit. | Daily |
| Relative momentum 7 | Of those four coins, enter up to two with positive trailing 7-day returns, at 45% each. A held coin can stay while in the top three; held weights can drift. | Weekly |
| Donchian 20/10 | On each coin, buy a new close above the preceding 20-day high and sell a close below the preceding 10-day low; allocate 25% on entry and let it drift until exit. | Daily |

Cash, BTC buy-and-hold, and equal-weight buy-and-hold are controls. Time-series momentum has evidence in diversified futures, and cryptocurrency research reports momentum and other factors; neither result establishes a CoinDCX INR spot edge after this account's costs. Channel breakouts and relative momentum test slower forms of those ideas. Sources: [Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen, *Time Series Momentum*](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Time-Series-Momentum), [Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu, *Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency*](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25882), and [Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen, *A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing*](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/A-Century-of-Evidence-on-Trend-Following-Investing).

An order is priced at the next hour's open, plus the pair's measured half-spread. Each side pays the configured regular CoinDCX INR spot fee and GST. A winning sale accrues estimated Indian VDA tax without netting losses, and tax owed is reserved from future purchases. Open positions are valued after hypothetical sale costs. Each reported order is checked *afterward* against the next hour's traded value; that future volume never changes the decision or cancels a loss. The whole test runs again with twice the half-spread. These assumptions are described in [CoinDCX's current spot-fee page](https://coindcx.com/fees/), [CoinDCX's candle API](https://docs.coindcx.com/), and [India's Income-tax Act, 2025, section 194(1), table row 4](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf). The user's actual tax jurisdiction, applicable cess/surcharge, TDS threshold and fee tier have not been established; the model is an estimate, not a tax calculation for that account.

This is a **retrospective screen**, not an untouched holdout: earlier research in this repository has already inspected parts of the same market period. A candidate must at least show positive mean and median after costs, profit in 12 of 17 windows, beat BTC holding in 9, and have no zero-volume or over-10%-of-hour orders before a forward paper trial could support even a small live pilot. Passing this arbitrary screen would still not prove an edge. The current [readiness screen](../docs/live-readiness.md) and separate exchange-order/reconciliation checks also apply.

Pairs trading and delta-neutral funding carry need real CoinDCX futures prices, funding and short-side execution data. Market making needs historical depth, queue position and adverse-selection data. The current hourly candles cannot validate these ideas, however profitable they look in other markets: see the original [pairs-trading study](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/19/3/797/1645003), [Avellaneda and Stoikov's market-making model](https://math.nyu.edu/faculty/avellane/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf), and the [BIS study of crypto carry risks](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.htm). The repository's earlier [CoinDCX research](../docs/research.md) already tried grids, dip-buying, fast trend rules, pump-chasing, 3–7-day momentum rotation, funding signals and machine learning; none established an after-cost live edge.

Reproduce with `python3 -m research.strategy_screen --history-dir /path/to/local/history`. The result JSON records each window, orders, drawdown, estimated tax, fill warnings, a doubled-spread stress case and a hash of the input candles. The 206 MB candle archive is intentionally excluded from Git.
